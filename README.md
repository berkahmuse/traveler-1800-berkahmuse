## The sentence

> Picture one road joining every library, post office, and market stall on earth, so a letter you send arrives before your ink dries and the book you seek is in your hands at once.

178 characters. One image a person from 1800 already knows — a road joining the library, the post office, and the market stall — carrying the whole of the modern internet: letters that arrive before the ink dries, and any book in your hands at once.

## How it works

`main.py` is deterministic: it writes the sentence above, byte for byte, to `/output/entry.txt`, checks the gate itself (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace), and prints the character count and sha256 of what it wrote.

```sh
python3 main.py
# wrote /output/entry.txt: 178 chars, sha256 <digest>
```
